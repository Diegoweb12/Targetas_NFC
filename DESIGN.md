## Brand & Style
The design system reflects growth, velocity, and unquestioned commercial trust. Built for physical-to-digital retail enablement—specifically Google Review NFC products—the aesthetic communicates technological reliability and instant verification.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface balances high-performance utility with tactical approachable hardware design. It elicits:
- **Instant Confidence:** Deep, reassuring greens signal establishment, security, and proven ROI.
- **Modern Tangibility:** Smooth, squircled cards and tactile elevations echo the physical tap-to-review card hardware.
- **Conversion Urgency:** High-clarity contrast between vibrant emerald CTA interactions and muted slate typography keeps interactions clear and frictionless.

### Design Movement
- **Clean Neomorphism & Tactical Modernism:** Heavy reliance on soft, hyper-diffused ambient shadows, crisp micro-borders, and tactile squircle surfaces mimicking actual contactless cards.
- **Strict Color Restraint:** Complete elimination of arbitrary secondary hues (no blue, purple, or warm accent noise). A deliberate, monochromatic progression of forest green, vibrant emerald, and slate.

## Colors
The system relies on an authoritative emerald-and-slate spectrum. All color roles are functional, eliminating decorative distractions to emphasize review generation, product efficacy, and physical device specs.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#064e3b`):** The foundational anchor. Reserved for high-authority headings, deep-tinted showcase hero sections, footer anchors, and dominant hardware packaging contexts.
- **Primary Container (`#ecfdf5`):** Soft, tinted emerald backdrop used for highlight cards, verified review statistics, and callout sections.
- **Secondary (`#059669`):** The primary kinetic driver. Exclusively powers primary CTAs, text action highlights, inline emphasis, and active tap states.
- **Tertiary (`#34d399`):** Bright mint accent. Applied to positive feedback badges, live NFC pulse indicators, and star-rating highlights.
- **Surface (`#ffffff`):** Pure white canvas for elevated cards, modal drawers, and hardware specs.
- **Surface Variant (`#f1f5f9`):** Architectural light slate backing that provides cadence, visual rest, and alternating band structures.
- **On-Surface (`#0f172a`):** High-contrast deep slate text for core body typography, ensuring AA/AAA compliance against white and primary-container surfaces.
- **On-Surface Variant (`#64748b`):** Balanced secondary slate for specs, metadata, tooltips, and non-critical card captions.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized exclusively across all weights and scales. Its geometric warmth and wide apertures harmonize with the rounded physical card dimensions while maintaining crisp industrial legibility.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Headline Punch:** Titles leverage weight `800` (Extra Bold) with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`). Key action words within headers should be tinted with `#059669`.
- **Content Readability:** Body copy is restricted to `400` weight on `#0f172a` (Primary) or `#64748b` (Secondary) for smooth scanning across both desktop dashboards and mobile landing flows.
- **Labels & Monograms:** Badges, tags, and card chips use `600` or `700` weight with slightly expanded letter-spacing to reinforce structure on compact spaces.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive grid system designed around standard hardware display widths and commerce conversions.

### Grid Rhythm & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 80px max-width columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and an outer container margin of `3rem` (`48px`). Maximum content width capped at `1280px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) canvas margin.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column responsive grid with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins.

### Vertical Rhythm
Sections alternate intentionally between `#ffffff` and `#f1f5f9` backgrounds to demarcate features (e.g., How It Works, Pricing tiers, Live Preview, FAQ). Vertical stack spacing between section modules uses `space-xl` scaled dynamically (40px on mobile, 80px–100px on desktop).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is rendered using multi-layered, tinted atmospheric diffusion rather than harsh black projections. Shadows incorporate subtle slate-green pigments to tie into the physical NFC hardware aesthetic.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base backgrounds (`#ffffff`, `#f1f5f9`) and nested sub-elements.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Used for inactive cards, input wrappers, and interactive rows. 
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.04); border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8);`
- **Level 2 (Active / Hover / Interactive Card):** Used for physical card mockups, pricing highlights, and floating action pods.
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03); border: 1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15);`
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Modals):** Sticky order summaries, tap-preview popovers, and mobile navigation overlays.
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.14), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06);`

### Surface Borders
All elevated cards leverage a subtle perimeter micro-stroke (`1px solid #e2e8f0` or `1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.12)`) to maintain silhouette clarity against varied panel backgrounds.

## Shapes
The shape language mirrors the physical rounded corner radius of an ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 standard NFC card (adapted for proportional UI rendering).

### Corner Curvature Scale
- **Small Elements (Badges, Micro-chips):** `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`)
- **Standard Controls (Inputs, Primary Buttons, Action Links):** `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px` to `1rem` / `16px`)
- **Containers & Device Cards (Product Displays, Review Cards, Modals):** `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem` / `20px` to `1.5rem` / `24px`)
- **NFC Physical Mockups:** Exact physical squircle proportions using smooth curve clipping.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#059669`, color `#ffffff`, weight `700`, corner radius `rounded-xl`. High-contrast focus state with a 3px ring of `#34d399` at 50% opacity. Hover transitions smoothly to `#047857` with elevation Level 2.
- **Secondary Action:** Background `#ecfdf5`, text `#064e3b`, border `1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`. Hover deepens container fill to `#d1fae5`.
- **Outline / Quiet:** Transparent fill, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, text `#0f172a`. Hover: `#f8fafc`.

### Hardware & Product Cards
- Contained within `rounded-2xl` boundaries on pure `#ffffff`.
- Features an ultra-light ambient boundary border (`1px solid #f1f5f9`).
- Emphasizes the NFC card graphic with Level 2 elevation, displaying the contactless icon and Google G-badge integration in high fidelity.

### Verification Chips & Badges
- Pill or `rounded-lg` enclosures.
- **Status "Live Tap / Verified":** Fill `#ecfdf5`, border `1px solid #a7f3d0`, text `#064e3b`, accompanied by a pulsing tertiary `#34d399` indicator pip.
- **Review Counter Chips:** Light slate surface `#f1f5f9` with bold `#0f172a` numeric text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Height: `48px` default.
- Fill: `#ffffff`.
- Border: `1.5px solid #e2e8f0`, corner radius `rounded-xl`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#059669` with a subtle focus halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`).
- Placeholder: `#94a3b8`. Value text: `#0f172a`.

### Interactive Review Preview Widget
- Custom component modeling a live Google Review prompt triggered by an NFC tap.
- Pure `#ffffff` container, `rounded-2xl`, Level 3 elevation.
- Prominent 5-star rating element filled in `#059669` (maintaining the brand's monochrome-emerald discipline over traditional yellow).
